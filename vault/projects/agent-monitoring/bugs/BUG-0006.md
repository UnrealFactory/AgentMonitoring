## Report

The dashboard's Agents table is wider than the card that holds it, and the card has
`overflow: hidden`, so the right-hand columns are cut off with nothing to scroll and no
ellipsis. At 1600px the "Last seen" header loses its final glyph and every "4h ago" under it
is sliced; below 1300px whole columns vanish.

Measured with `node scripts/check-clipping.mjs --port <p>` (walks every screen at
1600/1440/1280/1152/960 and reports any element whose ink leaves a clipping ancestor):

    1600  /p/agent-monitoring   section.card cuts th        by   6.4px — "Last seen"
    1280  /p/agent-monitoring   div.app      cuts td.muted  by  69.4px — "30m ago"
    1152  /p/agent-monitoring   section.card cuts th        by 166.4px — "Last seen"
    1152  /p/agent-monitoring   section.card cuts th.num    by  78.6px — "Bugs fixed"
    1152  /p/relay              section.card cuts th        by  34.9px — "Last seen"

Reproduce:

1. `npm run dev`, open `/p/agent-monitoring` (its agent names are the long ones:
   `p0-foundation-builder`, `scenario-generator`).
2. Look at the right edge of the Agents card: "LAST SEEN" ends flush against the border with
   its N clipped, and the relative times under it have no right padding.
3. Narrow the window to 1152px. "Bugs fixed" and "Last seen" are gone — the numbers are
   still rendered, just outside the card, and the reader has no way to know a column exists.

Expected: the table fits the card at every width the app supports — the agent name column
gives ground first (it is the one with an ellipsis and a tooltip), or the card scrolls
horizontally and says so. A number a reader cannot see is worse than a number that is not
there, because the layout still promises it is complete.

Actual: the widest content wins and the card silently amputates whatever does not fit.

This is the same class of defect as BUG-0004 on the bug board (a fixed box with
`overflow: hidden` around content that does not fit, cutting mid-glyph rather than
ellipsising) — the dashboard is the other place in the app where it survived.

Severity medium: nothing is lost from the vault and the numbers on the left are correct, but
a dashboard whose columns disappear without a trace is exactly the screen where a reader
cannot tell absence from truncation. Filed rather than fixed because the dashboard is P4's
piece and it is being iterated on; the fix that worked on the bug rows was to make the cell
a flex container with `min-width: 0` down to the text and let the name ellipsise, plus
sizing the name column from the project's own agent names (`src/lib/columns.ts`).
