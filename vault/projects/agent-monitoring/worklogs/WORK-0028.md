## What

The burn-up chart's tooltip prints the number on the side Korean puts it, and stops
breaking the word beside it in half.

`TipRow` and the tooltip's gap line hard-coded value-then-label. In Korean that reads
"11 시작 · 11 완료 · 0 진행 중" over a legend, 200px below and drawn from the same numbers,
reading "시작 27 · 완료 27 · 진행 중 0" — and over a spoken status for the *same bucket*
reading "시작 11, 완료 11, 진행 중 0". One chart, one word, two orders.

The swap that fixes it was already in this file, 150 lines below the bug, written for the
legend in round 1 under a comment explaining why. It is now a function, `counted(value,
label)`, and the legend, the tooltip's rows and the tooltip's gap line all call it. There is
one place that decides which side of the word the number is on.

Rendering the tooltip to gate it turned up a second defect in the same element: the row's
three flex items were all shrinkable, and a tip pinned near the right edge of its plot is
laid out in the room left of that edge — so it comes out at its 168px floor, the label was
handed 19px for a 21px word, and Korean broke it. 시작 became 시 over 작, one syllable per
line. `.tip-value` and `.tip-label` no longer shrink (`.tip-added` is the part that gives; it
has had an ellipsis for exactly this since it was written) and both carry `word-break:
keep-all`, the rule the vault bar's cells already carry.

`check:i18n` gains the question nothing in this repo asked, in two halves:

* **A bare numeral may not stand in front of one of the app's own state words.** Read from
  the dictionary, Korean only, measured in the page: a Range is laid over the match and must
  come back on one line with no gap between its pieces wider than a word space, or it is two
  things that happen to share a row. Groups never cross a control, a list item or a cell,
  and a display figure — a 48px stat over a 12px caption — is a figure and its caption, not
  a phrase.
* **The elements that carry a count and its word as two siblings must have them in the
  language's order** — label first in ko, value first in en. Structural, both languages, and
  deliberately the trio the critic caught disagreeing on one card: the legend, the tip rows,
  the tip gap.

The word-integrity sweep also carries the app's own state words as whole tokens now, beside
the 의존명사 it already carried, and the sweep opens the chart tooltips at all nine widths.

## Why

The P9 round 5 critic:

> The chart's tooltip prints the English word order in Korean — the one defect this app
> already named, fixed once, and left standing one element away. […] Every gate is blind to
> it by construction, and the new one too: check:i18n asks which *language* a string is in
> (시작 is Korean), VOCABULARIES asks whether a pill equals a dictionary value (it does,
> exactly), the round-5 word-integrity sweep asks whether a line break parted a number from
> its counter (nothing wrapped), and check:clipping measures overflow (nothing overflows).
> Nothing in this repo asks which side of the word the number is on — which is the one
> question the legend was fixed to answer.

That is the whole of it. Round 1 fixed one element and left the rule at the call site, so
the rule could only be right where somebody had remembered to type it. Four rounds of gates
walked past the tooltip because no gate had ever rendered it: it exists only while a chart
is hovered or focused, so it is in no screenshot, no DOM walk and no clipping run — the
surface a mouse reader meets most was the one surface nothing looked at.

Two rules follow from that and are now in the file:

* A rule that can be typed per element will eventually be typed wrong at one of them. It
  goes in a function.
* A gate that only reads the resting state of a screen is not reading the screen. The tips
  are opened, by pointer *and* by arrow key, on both charts of both projects, at two ranges
  and at nine widths.

The display-figure exemption is a real distinction and not a hole: the NOW panel's 48px 0
over a 12px "진행 중 · 전체 작업 로그 27개" is a stat tile, a register Korean sets exactly as
English does, with the grammatical sentence carried on the tile's own tooltip ("작업 로그
27개 중 0개 진행 중"). The phrases measure 1.1–1.2× the word beside them, the figures
2.7–4×; the gate cuts at 1.5×, in the empty middle.

## How

Mutation-tested in both directions, because a gate that has never failed is a gate nobody
has read:

* Restore the hard-coded value-then-label in `TipRow` and the gap line → the Korean sweep
  fails at every one of the nine widths, 10 findings at 1440 alone: the text half naming
  “1 진행 중” and “6등록”, the structural half naming all four rows and both gap lines.
* Force `[label, value]` for both languages → `--locale en` fails on every dashboard, chart
  tip, palette and toggled screen: 6 findings per screen with the tips shut, 12 with them
  open.
* Take `flex: none` and `keep-all` back off `.tip-label` → the sweep reports 시작 and 완료
  broken across two lines at the tooltip's own label.

Verified at the widths the critic named. Korean, 1440, both tips open at once (chart 0 by
arrow key, chart 1 by pointer, since a hover does not move focus): the 작업 tip reads
"시작 27 · 완료 27 · 진행 중 0" above a legend reading "시작 27 · 완료 27 · 진행 중 0"; the
버그 tip reads "등록 10 · 해결됨 9 · 미해결 1" above "등록 22 · 해결됨 21 · 미해결 1".
English at the same instant is unchanged: "27 started · 27 done · 0 in progress".

Green: `npm run build`, `check:i18n` ko (103 screens × 2 viewports + 45 sweep readings at
960/1060/1190/1200/1280/1360/1440/1520/1600) and en, `check:clipping` ko and en (546 screen
loads each), `check:keys`, `check:errors`, `check:urlstate`, `check:counts`, `check:live`,
`check:vault`, `smoke`, `check:mcp`, `cargo test`.

Not changed, on purpose: this is a closing round. The NOW panel's stat tiles, the work
list's filter tabs and the day-mix line were all read by the new assertion and all pass —
`dash.countPart` has kept both orders in the dictionary since it was written.
