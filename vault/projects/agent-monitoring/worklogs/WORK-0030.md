## What

The desktop window has no frame of its own any more, and the app draws what used to stand
there — 34px of empty dark strip with three buttons at the right end.

* `decorations: false`, `shadow: true` in src-tauri/tauri.conf.json. The shadow keeps Win11's
  rounded corners and drop shadow on an undecorated window. Nothing about the *title* changed:
  `set_title` still runs on every navigation (src/lib/useWindowTitle.ts), so the taskbar and
  Alt+Tab still read "작업 · AgentMonitoring · AgentMonitoring".
* src/components/Titlebar.tsx (new). `data-tauri-drag-region` on the strip — drag moves the
  window, double-click maximises it — and three `<button>`s: minimise, maximise/restore, close.
  12px-grade inline SVG on a 46 × 34 hit target, glyphs at `--text-secondary`, hover
  `--bg-active` for the two on the left and `--red` with a white glyph for close, focus a 1px
  inset accent hairline rather than the app's 2px offset ring, which does not fit a 34px strip.
* The middle button follows the *window*, not the click: `isMaximized()` at mount and on every
  `onResized`, so Win+↑, a snap, a drag off the top and a double-click all flip the glyph.
* Rendered only when `isTauri()` (src/App.tsx). Browser mode renders no strip and its shell
  keeps `class="app"` exactly; the strip costs the layout one rule,
  `.app.has-titlebar { height: calc(100vh - var(--titlebar-height)) }`, with the 34px in
  tokens.css beside the other layout numbers.
* Four capability grants by name in src-tauri/capabilities/default.json:
  `core:window:allow-start-dragging`, `allow-minimize`, `allow-toggle-maximize`, `allow-close`.
  No blanket window grant.
* Four dictionary keys in both languages: `window.minimize` / `window.maximize` /
  `window.restore` / `window.close` — 최소화 · 최대화 · 이전 크기로 · 닫기, and Minimize ·
  Maximize · Restore down · Close.

## Why

The ask, in the reader's words: *"the native Windows title bar (icon + '프로젝트 ·
AgentMonitoring' text) clashes with the app. Replace it with a frameless, integrated title bar
like modern Linear-class desktop apps: a slim (~34px) EMPTY dark strip that blends with the app
— no app name text, no icon, nothing on the left — with only minimize / maximize-restore /
close on the right, drawn in the app's own design language."*

It clashed because it was not this app: Segoe UI on a grey band, an icon, and a sentence the
sidebar and the screen already say twice over, sitting on top of a dark window that spends
every other pixel on being calm. A title bar is the one piece of chrome a desktop app cannot
opt out of, so the only way to stop it shouting is to draw it.

Empty on the left is the whole point, and it is not a shortcut: the app's name is in the
sidebar, the location is the page heading, and the window title — the copy that actually needs
words — lives in the taskbar, where the reader looks when the window is *not* in front of them.

## How

The strip is one piece with the app because it is painted like the app: `background:
var(--bg-app)`, and a `::before` that is exactly the sidebar's column — `var(--sidebar-width)`
wide, `var(--bg-sidebar)`, `border-right: 1px solid var(--border-subtle)` — so the sidebar's
colour and its hairline run unbroken from y=0. A pseudo-element rather than a child on purpose:
Tauri's drag script (tauri/src/window/scripts/drag.js) walks the elements under the pointer, a
bare `data-tauri-drag-region` means *this element only*, and a real child there would have been
a hole in the drag region. The same rule is why the three buttons are direct children of the
strip and not in a wrapper — that script already refuses to drag from a `<button>`.

The permissions were read out of src-tauri/gen/schemas/acl-manifests.json rather than guessed:
`core:window:default` (bundled by `core:default`) already carries `allow-is-maximized` and
`allow-internal-toggle-maximize` — the second is what a double-click on a drag region invokes —
so only the four commands the buttons and the drag actually call are added. A denied command is
reported rather than swallowed (`console.warn` naming the missing permission), because this repo
has already lost a round to a silently rejected `set_title`.

The strip sits at `z-index: 90`, above every layer in app.css (menus 30/40, palette 60, context
menu 70, toast 80). A modal that covered the window buttons would leave a window that cannot be
moved or closed until it is dismissed, which is not something an app gets to decide.

If this strip ever needs a gate of its own, that stub is the shape it would take.

## Outcome

Verified in the real WebView2 window (`npm run tauri:dev`), by driving and photographing it,
not by reading the code.

The window: opens 1440 × 900 client with no frame, rounded corners and a shadow intact. Drag
the strip and it moves (+40, +30 asked, +40, +30 got). Double-click it and it maximises to
2560 × 1392 — the work area, taskbar still visible, nothing off-screen — and the middle glyph
flips to the two-sheet restore mark; double-click again and it comes back to 1440 × 900. The
buttons: maximise, restore and minimise each did their job on click (`IsZoomed`/`IsIconic`
read back off the window). Resizing works from every edge and both corners tested, by real
drags: right +80, left +80 (origin moved), bottom +70, top (grew upward at 0, 2 and 4px into
the strip — the OS keeps the top edge, the drag region starts under it), bottom-right
−110 × −80, top-left −75 × −284. A restart from a cold config change opened at 1440 × 900
again, and the window title read "작업 · AgentMonitoring · AgentMonitoring" — `set_title`
still runs with no decorations to draw it.

The strip, photographed: at 1440 and at 1110 the top 60px is empty except the three glyphs, and
at 6× magnification the sidebar's hairline runs from y=0 through the strip into the shell with
no step and no change of colour. Hover states: 최소화 and 최대화 lighten by
`--bg-active`; close turns `--red` with a white glyph and follows the rounded corner. Tooltips
read 최소화, 최대화, 닫기 in Korean and Maximize in English after the toggle. 1440, 1110 and 960
(the configured floor) all draw the shell under the strip with nothing clipped and the sidebar's
top spacing unchanged; with the command palette open the strip stays undimmed and its buttons
live, above the scrim.

The DOM, measured in a browser told to be Tauri (`__TAURI_INTERNALS__` stubbed, so the
maximised label can be read without maximising): strip 34px tall, full width, at y=0, z-index
90, drag attribute present; rail 232px of rgb(11,12,14) against a 232px sidebar; three buttons
of 46 × 34 at 92/46/0 from the right edge; labels 최소화 / 최대화 / 닫기, 이전 크기로 when
maximised, Minimize / Restore down / Close in English; `.app` = "app has-titlebar", y=34,
height = viewport − 34 at 960, 1440 and 1600, and `document.scrollHeight` equal to the viewport
at each, so nothing is displaced or pushed under a scrollbar. The same page without the stub:
no `.titlebar` in the document and `class="app"` exactly — browser mode is untouched.

Gates on this build: build (tsc + vite) green, smoke 373 green, check:keys 126 green,
check:i18n ko and en green (the four new keys are covered in both), check:clipping at
1600/1152/960 green in both languages.
