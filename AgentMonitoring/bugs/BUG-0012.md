## Report

The dashboard's range control does not change the burn-up legends. At `?range=7d`, under a
sentence that says "The charts, the agents and the feed below cover the last 7 days", the Work
card prints `12 started · 10 finished or abandoned · 2 in flight` and the Bugs card prints
`8 filed · 6 fixed · 2 still open` — the project-lifetime totals, byte-identical to All time —
while everything else on the page does rescope (the Agents table drops nova from 30 events to
12, the feed from 85 events to 35).

Reproduce:

1. `npm run dev`, open `/p/relay`, note the two legends.
2. Click **7 days**. The sentence under the control changes. The six numbers in the two legends
   do not.
3. Check what the seven days actually hold:
   `agentmon work list -p relay --json` — 3 started (WORK-0012, WORK-0011, WORK-0010) and 3
   finished or abandoned (WORK-0010, WORK-0009, WORK-0007); `agentmon bug list -p relay --json`
   — 4 filed and 3 fixed.

Expected: a control that scopes the page scopes the charts too, and says so in numbers. This is
not a demand that the *lines* be redrawn from zero — a burn-up is cumulative, and its level at
the right edge is the project's lifetime total whatever window it is drawn in; the level is the
honest thing to plot. What is missing is the other half: what changed *inside* the window, which
is the only number the range control can move.

Actual: a reader who clicks 7 days sees a control that appears to do nothing to the two panels
it sits directly above, and a legend that contradicts the sentence beside it.

Severity medium: no number shown is false — 12 work logs really have been started in this
project — but a filter that visibly changes nothing teaches a reader to distrust it, and this
one sits between a sentence that says "the last 7 days" and two charts that answer a different
question.

Found by the P4 design critic in round 1 (progress/rounds.jsonl, P4 round 1, verdict fail),
listed there as the second defect after the axis labelling (BUG-0011).

## Resolution

**Root cause.** Both burn-ups are cumulative, so every point is a running total and the last
point is the project's lifetime total — by construction, in every window. The legend printed that
last point. It was therefore correct, unchanged by the range control, and (correctly) identical
at 7 days and at All time. Nothing was computing the number the control *does* move.

The data for it was already there: `cumulative` records `baseUpper` and `baseLower`, the levels
the two lines stood at when the window opened, because the lines have to start from the right
height rather than from zero.

**Fix, in three parts.**

1. `BurnUp` takes a `scope` (the window's name, "the last 7 days", or null for All time). Given
   one, each legend entry carries `total − base` beside its total as a signed chip — `+3`, `−1`,
   `±0` — with the period in its tooltip and in the chart's accessible summary. At All time the
   chips are absent, because there the total *is* the change and a `+12` beside a `12` is noise.
   The card's subtitle names the period once: "Started against finished, running total, with the
   change over the last 7 days".

2. Those figures were then wrong by one, which is the part of this fix worth reading. Buckets are
   floored to their own step so that ticks land on clock boundaries (BUG-0011), and that floor
   reaches *backwards* — up to a whole step before "seven days ago". Everything the axis scopes
   counts from `axis.from`, so the seven-day window was quietly twelve hours wider than seven days
   and picked up WORK-0008, finished at 11 Aug 14:20Z against a window that opened at 11 Aug
   22:21Z. The legend said `+4 finished`; the vault says 3. `timeAxis` now clips the first bucket
   back to the requested start — except when the window was deliberately widened to keep a
   young project's chart from being three marks wide, where clipping would invert the bucket.

3. The same `axis.from` scopes the Agents table and the Activity feed, so those two rescoped by
   the same twelve hours and are now exact as well.

**Why this is the right shape.** Redrawing the lines from zero inside the window would have been
the other option, and it lies in a subtler way: it would draw relay's eight-bug backlog as though
four bugs existed, and the gap between the lines — the thing this chart is read for — would stop
being "how many are still open". The level is the honest thing to plot; the change is the honest
thing to print beside it.

**Verified** against the CLI on the live vault, at the same instant the page was rendered
(window 11 Aug 22:21Z → 18 Aug 22:21Z):

    started in window                WORK-0012, WORK-0011, WORK-0010          → legend +3
    finished or abandoned in window  WORK-0010, WORK-0009, WORK-0007          → legend +3
    filed in window                  BUG-0008, BUG-0007, BUG-0006, BUG-0005   → legend +4
    fixed in window                  BUG-0004, BUG-0005, BUG-0006             → legend +3

and the gap chips read `±0 in flight` and `+1 still open`, which is 12−10 against 9−7 and 8−6
against 4−3.

`scripts/check-urlstate.mjs` now asserts the control moves the numbers: `?range=7d` must render
six legend deltas of which at least one is not `±0`, and the default All-time view must render
none. `npm run build`, `check:clipping` (50 screens × 7 widths), `smoke` (102) and
`check:counts` (618) are all clean on this build.
