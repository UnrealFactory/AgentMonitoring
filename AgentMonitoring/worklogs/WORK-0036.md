## What

Add shared agent notes end to end. Core: a `notes/<name>.md` record in agentmon-core
(model, store, write, doctor) with kebab-case name-as-identity, four types
(memory/handoff/decision/reference), a required one-line description, free-form bodies,
and three new events (note_created/note_updated/note_removed). CLI: `agentmon note
add/update/remove/list/view` with `--search`. App: a Notes list (type tabs, agent/tag
filters, URL state, keyboard) and a Note detail page, plus notes in the sidebar, palette,
related index, feed verbs/icons/tones, per-agent split bar and both i18n dictionaries.
Browser mode: the JS twin (scripts/project-fs.mjs) serves /notes and counts them in the
cursor. MCP: one `note` tool (list/read/write-upsert/remove). Docs: SPEC.md schema,
AGENT_MANUAL.md section + Recipe 4, MCP.md.

## Why

Agents already keep memory and handoffs — in scratch files, in chat, nowhere. The owner
asked for a place for them: agents write and explore it, humans read it in the app,
Claude-Code-memory style (an index of one-line descriptions, one fact per file, update
over duplicate). Work logs could not carry this: they are append-only history, and
knowledge that has gone stale misleads every reader, so the new kind had to be mutable
and removable — which is exactly why it could not be bolted onto the existing records.

## How

The design hinge is records-vs-knowledge: worklogs/bugs stay append-only and undeletable;
a note is rewritten in place and may be removed, with every move appended to events.jsonl
as the audit trail. Identity is the kebab name (file name, URL, refs value) rather than a
NOTE-NNNN sequence, because notes are addressed by topic; names that look like record ids
and Windows device names are rejected. refs now accept note names, checked against the
notes that exist at write time so a typo fails loudly. The feed's new series colour was
not eyeballed: the dataviz validator ran against the real adjacent pairs, teal failed
against the done-green (dE 5.7) and magenta #c9629b passed, with the strips ordered so a
note segment never touches the fix-purple or the neutral grey.

## For humans

The owner asked for one place where agents keep what they know. Until now they kept it in scratch files, in chat, or nowhere at all.

**There is now a third kind of record, beside work logs and bug reports.** A note is one file, `notes/<name>.md`, named after its subject, carrying a one-line description and then whatever text is useful. Four kinds: a memory, a handoff for whoever picks the work up next, a decision, a reference.

`agentmon note` adds, updates, removes, lists, views and searches them, and in the app they get two screens of their own. When another record points at a note, that pointer is checked as it is written, so a typo fails loudly.

**The point of a new kind is that this one may be rewritten.** A work log or a bug report is history: it can never be changed or taken away. Knowledge is not. A note gone out of date misleads everybody who reads it, so it is corrected in place and thrown away when it stops being true. Every add, edit and removal still goes into `events.jsonl`, the running log of everything that happens in a project.

It is like a whiteboard beside a filing cabinet: you wipe the board, and the cabinet still holds the note saying you wiped it.

**Agents that never open a terminal reach notes too.** They talk to this app through a go-between that hands them a short list of tools, and notes are a sixth one: list, read, write, remove. Writing a name that already exists rewrites that note rather than refusing. That list of tools is sent to an agent again and again, so its size is capped; with the sixth aboard it measures 6,070 bytes of the 6,144 allowed. The browser version of the app reads notes through its own copy of the same rules, so both halves show the same list.

**The new colour on the activity strip was not chosen by eye.** The dataviz validator measured it against the colours it sits beside, which is what a colour checker is for: teal came out too close to the green already there and failed, and the magenta `#c9629b` passed. When such a check comes back no, a reader who cannot tell two colours apart reads a chart that says nothing.

**The Korean sweep found two real faults in this very feature.** `check:i18n` loads every screen in Korean and reads it, 141 of them each time. It caught two new surfaces where Korean broke in the middle of a word, and a hint that glued a Korean grammatical ending onto a code snippet. Both were fixed and the sweep repeated. `cargo test --workspace`, every test written against this project's Rust code, came back clean three times running.

**What is different: agents share one memory, and you can read it in the app.** Removing is the one thing an agent may do here and never to a work log or a bug, and `SPEC.md`, the written contract this app is built to, now carries that exception.

Knowledge nobody is allowed to correct stops being knowledge.
