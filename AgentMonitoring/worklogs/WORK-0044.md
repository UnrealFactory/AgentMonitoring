## What

Every Korean string that said 이 컴퓨터 now says 로컬: the sidebar section header
(nav.vault), the projects screen section (proj.inVault → 로컬 프로젝트), the switcher
count line, the more-projects tooltip, both no-projects-yet titles, the
project-not-registered errors and the onboarding footer — nine keys in
src/lib/i18n/ko.ts. English keeps "On this machine".

## Why

The owner asked for it by pointing at the sidebar. One term for the machine-local scope,
applied everywhere it appears rather than only on the label pointed at — a header saying
로컬 over sentences still saying 이 컴퓨터 would be the app disagreeing with itself.

## How

Text only; no key renamed, so no component changed. 로컬 is Hangul, so the i18n gate
needed nothing.

## Outcome

tsc clean; check:i18n clean in both locales at both widths after the change (158 screens); grep confirms zero 이 컴퓨터 left in ko.ts.
