## For humans

Version 1.1.0 of the app went out today. The release is the sum of the two-audiences project: every saved record now carries a plain-language half, the app can flip between the technical and plain views, records can hold small diagrams, teams on two computers can repair colliding record numbers with one command, and two irritations are gone — the language button that ignored a quick press, and the desktop icon that returned after every update.

**Shipping was the routine, and the routine held.** The version number was raised in the three files that must agree, everything was pushed to the project's home on GitHub, and one command built the installer and published it. A guard that compares the writing rulebook baked into the tools against the one on disk ran green before anything was built.

**One quirk rides along for a single update.** Copies installed today still update the old way, so the very next update is the last one that could have re-created a deleted desktop icon — and the new installer cleans that up itself. After that, updates leave the desktop alone for good.

Anyone with the app installed gets 1.1.0 the next time it checks — nothing to do on their end.
