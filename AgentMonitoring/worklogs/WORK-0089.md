## What

Flip the human area's picture rule from opt-in to default-per-beat, and give doctor a sweep for the shape that cannot be the valve. docs/HUMAN_STYLE.md: the compact rules gain a scene line (every beat opens on its scene — an SVG under assets/, named `<record>-<beat>-<what>.svg`, cited as the beat body's first line; skip only where a beat's facts draw nothing, and the skip is a claim about the beat), and the two long-form picture bullets are rewritten from "reach for one where it earns its place" to the same default. agentmon-core: `human::beat_count` / `human::figure_count` (lead-in blocks and whole-line `![alt](src)` figures, fence-aware, mirroring `FIGURE_BLOCK` in src/lib/human.ts), and `doctor` warns — work logs and bugs only — when a human area has beats and not one figure anywhere. MCP budgets raised deliberately with the doc growth (primer 5800→6200, contract 29000→30500) and docs/MCP.md's published handover figure updated to "About 5,900 characters, once".

## Why

Owner decision, 2026-08-25, on reviewing the live pages: agents were drawing no scenes at all. The old wording — "a picture is one beat's scene, and it earns its place or it is decoration" — made the picture an option, and every writer took the option. The infrastructure existed (assets/, the check:scenes geometry gate, per-beat naming, three records with hand-drawn scenes) and went unused. Full per-beat enforcement was considered and rejected: the contract forbids invented panels, some beats honestly draw nothing (a check that passed, a number that moved), and a refusal would force decoration — the same false-positive trap WORK-0075 documented. So the default flipped, the valve stayed per beat, and doctor flags only the whole-page shape the valve cannot explain.

## How

The sweep is whole-page on purpose: `beat_count > 0 && figure_count == 0`. Doctor cannot judge which beat's facts draw nothing (that is the writer's per-beat claim), so demanding a figure per beat would demand the forbidden panel; a page of beats with zero pictures is the only shape that cannot be the valve, and it warns, never errors. Notes are excluded — knowledge, not chase stories. The first live run flags 109 records; they stay flagged until a refresh re-passes each page with its scenes added. WORK-0089's own human area is the first page written under the new default: two beats with drawn scenes, one verification beat honestly bare.

## For humans

The plain-language pages in this project were shipping with no pictures at all. The writing guide told agents to add a drawing "where it earns its place", and every writer read that as permission to skip — the drawing tools and the checks for them sat ready and unused. On 25 August 2026 the owner flipped the rule.

**Every beat opens on its picture now.**

![the same page before and after the default flipped](assets/work-0089-1-the-default-flipped.svg)

The guide — `docs/HUMAN_STYLE.md`, the contract an agent is shown the moment a page is refused — now makes the picture the default: each numbered block of a story starts with a small drawing of that block's own cast, drawn from its sentences and nothing else. Skipping is still allowed, but only where a beat's facts draw nothing — a check that ran and passed, a number that moved — and the skip is a claim about the beat, never about the time a drawing costs.

**A page of beats with no picture anywhere is now flagged.**

![the checker reads every page and flags the one with no scenes](assets/work-0089-2-the-checker-flags-bare-pages.svg)

`agentmon doctor` — the command that reads a whole project's records and lists what is untidy — gained the matching complaint. It stays a complaint, not a refusal: the checker cannot know which beat's facts draw nothing, so it flags only the shape the exception can never explain, a story told in beats with not one picture on it. Its first run here flagged 109 pages; each keeps the flag until someone adds its drawings.

**How we know, and why this beat carries no picture.** All 195 checks of the record engine and all 209 of the agent-facing tool server pass, the three copies of the writing rules are byte-identical again, and the drawing gate measured both new pictures at every width the app draws them. Those facts are checks that ran and passed — they draw nothing, so this beat stays bare: the new rule's own exception, used the way it is meant.

A rule that says "when it is worth it" is read as never; a rule that says "always, unless you can say why not" is read as always.
