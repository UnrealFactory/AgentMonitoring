The list of bugs in this app gave each bug two lines and a dot coloured for how bad it was. You could narrow it by that dot or by a label, and nothing else. The page for one bug printed the report, the comments and the fix as three blocks of equal weight. I rebuilt both screens.

**The code that draws a record was changing what the record says.** A work log called `WORK-0005` has a sentence that runs over the end of a line: "…returns 503 for three hours, then" / "200. Without jitter…". The drawing code saw a line starting with a number and a full stop. It took that for the opening of a numbered list, and printed "1." where the file says 200.

It is like a helper who tidies your handwriting, sees a figure at the top of a line, and renumbers the page for you.

**The rule is narrower now.** A line opening with a number may interrupt a paragraph only when that number is 1. I also pulled the working-out apart from the drawing, so the working-out can be tested on its own.

**A check with teeth.** `npm run smoke` feeds passages copied word for word out of real records through that working-out, and compares what comes out with what went in. It then sweeps every record in the project and fails if one word of a record is missing from what was drawn: 58 checks, all passing. A page that swallows a number is what it guards against, and the reader is the one person who cannot tell. I put the bad rule back to watch it fail, and it failed with the 200 gone.

**Rows used to give up their titles first.** At the width where a reviewer counted nine of twelve titles cut short, one is now cut, and it still shows 95 percent of itself. At the smallest window, where one title had been worn to a single capital T, none now falls below two thirds of itself. A row sheds its tag chips first, then the agent's name.

**A link between records has two ends now.** Records point at other records, and the page only showed the pointers going out. Each record now also lists every record pointing at it, so the work somebody filed off the back of yours shows on your page.

Still open: the tabs on the list of work logs count the whole project while the rows beneath them are filtered. That one is filed as `BUG-0005`, for another round to fix.

A page that quietly improves what you wrote has stopped showing you your record.
