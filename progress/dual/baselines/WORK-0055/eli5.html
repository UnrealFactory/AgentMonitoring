How it works — like a magic trick 🪄

🕵️
1. Invisible helper A hidden worker does the real job: download, install, restart the app. You never see it.
🎭
2. Pretty curtain The splash card is just decoration — it covers the gap while the old app closes and the new one opens.
🎉
3. Ta-da! The moment the fresh new app appears, the curtain sees it and quietly removes itself.

And if something goes wrong? 🛟

💬
Friendly popup The curtain drops and a normal message box tells you the update failed — no cryptic console.
📓
Secret diary The hidden worker writes everything it did to a log file, so a human can check later.
⏲️
3-minute rule The card can never get stuck forever — after 3 minutes it gives up and closes itself.
