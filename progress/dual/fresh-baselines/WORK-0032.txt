The Delete Button
One app got a real delete — and only the human can press it.
📦🙈
Before: the Hide-It Box
Old projects went into an “archive” — hidden, but still there.
Nobody wanted it, and every screen needed special rules for it.
So the box was thrown out .
🏠 ➜ 💨
Now: really gone
Done with a project? Delete it, and its folder truly
leaves the disk . No hiding. Gone means gone.
Who can delete?
🧑‍💻🔑
The human — yes
In the app window, behind a confirmation.
🤖⛔
Robots — never
AI agents can write notes into the vault, but they have no delete button at all .

The guard checks the address
Before anything is removed, the app makes sure the folder is
really one of its own projects — sitting right inside its own house.
Sneaky addresses like “../..” that point somewhere else are refused.
