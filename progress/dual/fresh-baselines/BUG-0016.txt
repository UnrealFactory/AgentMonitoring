BUG-0016 · explained like you're five
The test that failed
once in 16 tries 👻
A ghost in the test suite — and how it got caught.
1
Something failed… once
just this one 😨
The tests ran 16 times . Fifteen were fine.
One failed. Then it never failed again.
2
And the note got torn ✂️
test result: FAILED.
20 passed; 1 failed
which one? it said,
right below… here ↓
the failing test's name
✂️
The computer's message was cut short to save space —
and the part naming which test failed was on the piece that got thrown away.
(The log was piped through tail -12 , which keeps only the last 12 lines.)
3
The suspect: a shared key 🔑
ONE AT
A TIME
🔑 .lock
writers waiting their turn…
Many little programs share one room (the project's files).
A lock file is the key on the door: whoever holds it goes in, everyone else waits .
4
Windows says the wrong thing 😠
USUALLY
BUSY
“Occupied — just wait 🙂”
FOR A SPLIT SECOND
NO ENTRY!!
“ACCESS DENIED 😠” …huh?!
While someone is halfway out the door , Windows doesn't say “busy, wait” —
it snaps “ACCESS DENIED!” even though the room is about to be free.
(A lock file being deleted is “delete-pending”: create_new gets PermissionDenied , not AlreadyExists .)
5
Our robot gave up 💥
Robot's rulebook 📖
“BUSY” → wait, retry ✔
“NO ENTRY” → ???
💥
test fails!
The robot knew how to handle “busy” : wait and try again.
But “no entry” wasn't in its rulebook — so it crashed instead of waiting.
That's why it was so rare: two robots had to swap the key at the exact same instant, which mostly happened when the disk was slow and busy.
6
The fix: one new rule 🩹
Rulebook v2 📖✨
“BUSY” → wait, retry ✔
“NO ENTRY” → wait, retry ✔
⏳
✅
waits a moment… gets in. every time.
Teach the robot: an angry “no entry” at the door just means
someone's still leaving — wait a moment and knock again.
( PermissionDenied on the lock file now retries exactly like AlreadyExists , with the same time limit. Fixed in BUG-0024.)
