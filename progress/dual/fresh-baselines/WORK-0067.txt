Step 4 · The fix
One rulebook for everyone
Write the rule for “what counts as a heading” once —
and make every reader follow the exact same rule the screen uses to draw.
📖
THE ONE RULE
If the screen would draw it as a heading,
the guard treats it as a heading.
Tabs, ghosts, fancy spaces, CAPITALS, extra colons — all count.
