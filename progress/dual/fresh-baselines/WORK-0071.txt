The bug before
Some old records have no human side — just an empty box with one button: “show the agent side instead.” That button secretly flipped the BIG switch. Your pick was gone, and nothing told you.

4
The fix after
Now that button is just a peek 👀. It shows the agent side of this one record only , tells you so with a little note, and your pin stays put. Walk to the next record — human side again.
